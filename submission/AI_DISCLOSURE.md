# AI disclosure

**Short version (for Devpost):**
> The idea, problem, sources, design decisions and testing are mine. The code was written with an AI coding assistant (Claude Code), and the demo video's voice-over is computer-generated (Kokoro text-to-speech). No data or results were AI-generated: every fact comes from the cited sources and OpenStreetMap.

**My work:** chose the problem (Adana's open canals, swimming access and the canal-closure plan); wrote the project brief and its honesty rules; found the starting sources (the municipality's swimming news, the council decision PDF and the ministry announcement); made the design decisions (study area, thresholds, which facilities count); and tested and reviewed the app.

**AI tool:** Claude Code (Anthropic) wrote the application code, tests and documentation drafts from my brief, and scripted the automated screen recording of the demo video. The narration voice is the open-source Kokoro text-to-speech model (voice “am_michael”), run locally; its sentences are checked against the numbers the app shows during recording.

**Not AI-generated:** no data, statistics, interviews or community responses were invented. Facts come from official sources and OpenStreetMap, listed in `docs/EVIDENCE.md`.
